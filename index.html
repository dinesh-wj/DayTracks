Day Tracks

Each day is a line of milestones. Each milestone is tracked by its start time. Your actual path rides the line when you start on time, dips below it when you start late, and drops to the bottom lane when you miss one. Click any milestone to log it.

Plan templates

The default plan is used on any day you haven't picked a template for. To pick one, use the menu beside a day's date. Past days keep the plan they had when you logged them.

Delete entries

This removes what you logged on those days, including flow blocks, distractions and wins, and syncs to all your devices. Your plan templates are kept. It can’t be undone, so download a backup first if you might want the entries back. To clear one day, use Clear day beside its date; to clear one milestone, click it and choose Clear.
